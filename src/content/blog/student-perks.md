Your college email ID (the one ending in `.ac.in`, `.edu.in` or `.edu`) is one of the most valuable things you get in college, and most students never use it. It proves you are a student, and that unlocks free developer tools, cloud credits and courses worth a lot of money. Claim them in your first week, because they disappear when you graduate.

> Offers change often. Everything below was checked on the official pages in September 2026. Always confirm the current terms on the linked page before you sign up.

## 1. GitHub Student Developer Pack

The single best perk for a developer. Apply at [education.github.com/pack](https://education.github.com/pack) with your college email or proof of enrollment. Once GitHub verifies you, you get a long list of partner offers, including:

| Offer | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| GitHub Pro | Free while you are a student |
| GitHub Copilot Student | Unlimited code completions and an allowance of AI credits, with limited chat and agent usage |
| GitHub Codespaces | Pro-level access to cloud dev environments |
| Microsoft Azure | $100 in Azure credit (for students aged 18+) |
| JetBrains IDEs | A free subscription, renewed every year |
| Namecheap | A free `.me` domain for one year, perfect for your portfolio |
| MongoDB | $50 in MongoDB Atlas credits |
| Heroku | $13 per month in credit for 24 months |
| Frontend Masters | 6 months of free access to all courses |
| Educative | 6 months of free access to 70+ courses |
| DataCamp | 3 months of free access |
| Datadog | Pro account with 10 servers, free for 2 years |
| Notion | The Notion Education plan with extra AI responses |
| Stripe | $25 in credits |
| Clerk | Free Pro plan while you are a student |

**How to use it well**: put your portfolio on the free `.me` domain, host a full-stack project with the MongoDB and cloud credits, and use Frontend Masters or Educative to fill gaps in your learning.

## 2. Notion Education Plus

Notion gives students and educators at accredited colleges its **Education Plus plan free**: unlimited pages and blocks, file uploads, more options to publish your site, and 30-day version history. Sign in with your college email, then choose **Get free Education plan** from the billing settings.

Use it for class notes, a placement-prep tracker, and a revision wiki. You can also publish your notes as a website, just like [our interview notes](/notes).

## 3. Azure for Students

Full-time university students get **$100 of Azure credit to use within 12 months, with no credit card needed**, plus free monthly amounts of many popular services. You can renew every year while you are a student. It's enough to deploy real projects, try databases and experiment with AI services.

## 4. Figma for Education

Verified students and teachers get **Figma Design and FigJam for free**. Create an account with your college email, verify your student status, and set up an education team to unlock the professional features. It's great for designing your project UI before you code it.

## 5. JetBrains IDEs

JetBrains' IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, WebStorm, CLion and the rest are **free for students with an annual renewal** (also part of the GitHub pack). If you write Java, C++ or Python for DSA, a professional IDE makes debugging much easier.

## Other smart uses of your college email

- **Cold emails to professors and recruiters**: a message from an official college address is far more likely to be opened than one from a random Gmail. Use it for research internship requests (see [research internships](/opportunities#research)).
- **Proof of student status**: many student programs, hackathons and discounts ask you to verify that you are enrolled, and your college email is the fastest way.
- **Check your college's own licences**: many colleges give students Microsoft 365, MATLAB or other software through their college account. Ask your IT department.

## Before you graduate

1. **Make a personal email your primary login** everywhere, and add your college email only for verification. College accounts are often deleted after you graduate.
2. **Export your data**: download your Notion workspace, Drive files and any certificates.
3. **Move domains and cloud projects** to a personal account before the credits run out.
